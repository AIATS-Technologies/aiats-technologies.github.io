Portfolio Manager is not the same as AIATS Partner.

Both models can involve multiple clients, but the operational responsibility and relationship with those clients are different.

AIATS Operating Principle

Separation between capital ownership and execution infrastructure.

AIATS is designed to operate as an algorithmic trading technology layer. Depending on the selected structure, capital may remain within the relevant client's, manager's, company's or fund's trading environment.

AIATS connects to the relevant trading environment and executes predefined strategies according to the configured operational model.

This separation allows the same underlying technology to support different business structures.
